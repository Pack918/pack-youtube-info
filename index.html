PERSONAL CHANNEL AUTOMATION

PACK YouTube

A privately operated local assistant for creating and scheduling engineering explainers on the How Stuff Holds Up YouTube channel.

What the app does

PACK researches public sources, drafts narration using local AI models, renders videos with MoneyPrinterTurbo, checks outputs, and uploads approved outputs to its owner's channel on a morning and evening schedule.

This is a personal application for its owner. It is not a public sign-up service and is not affiliated with Google or YouTube.

YouTube access

The owner signs in through Google to grant video-upload permission and read-only YouTube access. PACK uses these permissions to verify the intended channel and upload videos with titles, descriptions and scheduled publication times.
