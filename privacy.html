PACK YouTube

Privacy policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026.

PACK YouTube is a personal, locally operated application for the owner of How Stuff Holds Up. This policy describes its handling of Google and YouTube data.

Data accessed and purpose

With the owner's Google authorization, PACK receives OAuth tokens, reads channel identity to verify the destination, and receives upload identifiers and processing or publication status. It sends rendered videos, titles, descriptions, tags, audience declarations and scheduling information to YouTube to operate the owner's channel.

Storage and retention

Authorization tokens and client configuration are stored on the owner's Windows computer in an access-restricted directory excluded from source control. Scripts, research references, video files, queue records and upload receipts are stored locally. They remain until the owner removes them. Revoking access prevents future authorized requests but does not automatically delete local files or existing YouTube uploads.

Sharing and AI use

Videos and associated metadata are sent to YouTube as directed by the owner. Public research queries and stock-footage searches go to their configured search and media providers. The automatic script and sampled visual-review steps use local models. Google account data is not used to train generalized AI models, sold, or shared with advertisers. This static information page has no application login or analytics code; its hosting provider may retain ordinary web request logs.

Owner controls

The owner can pause PACK's queue, remove local records, manage uploaded videos in YouTube Studio, and revoke PACK's authorization at Google Account connections. To remove stored credentials, the owner can delete PACK's local token and client configuration files.

Contact

App operator: Pack918 on GitHub. The Google consent screen also provides the operator's support email.
